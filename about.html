    Our Story

    About KS Rent a Car Sri Lanka

    Delivering dependable transportation, well-maintained vehicles, and responsive customer support for journeys across Sri Lanka.

    Who We Are

    KS Rent a Car Sri Lanka (srilankacarrents.com) is a dedicated Sri Lankan car rental service founded on a straightforward mission: to provide visitors, returning expatriates, families, and businesses with reliable vehicles, fair terms, and helpful, direct communication.

    Exploring Sri Lanka should be a memorable, joyful experience. From the sunny shores of the southern coastline and historic fortresses of Galle to the mist-covered tea plantations of Nuwara Eliya and the sacred ruins of the Cultural Triangle, having dependable road transportation is vital. We take pride in ensuring each vehicle in our fleet is clean, road-tested, and ready for your travel itinerary.

    What Sets Us Apart

    Reliable Fleet Care

    Our vehicles—including popular models like the Toyota Premio, Allion, Vezel, and KDH passenger vans—undergo regular maintenance inspections before every rental.

    Direct Communication

    No confusing automated call centers. You chat directly with our team on WhatsApp (+94 77 719 3915) to discuss vehicle options, schedules, and quotes.

    Self-Drive & Chauffeur

    Whether you prefer the freedom of self-drive exploration or the relaxed comfort of an experienced driver, we accommodate your style.

    Flexible Arrangements

    From airport pickups at Bandaranaike International Airport to multi-week tour rentals and wedding car packages, we adapt to your plan.

    Islandwide Reach

    While our primary operations coordinate around Colombo, Katunayake (Bandaranaike International Airport), and Negombo, vehicle delivery and collection can be arranged across various destinations in Sri Lanka upon enquiry. Let us know where you plan to start and end your journey, and we will work with you to make the logistics straightforward.
