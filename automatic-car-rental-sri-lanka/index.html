Why Renting an Automatic Car Makes a Real Difference in Sri Lanka

Driving on unfamiliar roads with left-hand traffic, sudden elevation changes, and active town centers is significantly more enjoyable in an automatic vehicle.

No Clutch Fatigue

Stop-and-go driving in town centers like Colombo, Kandy, and Galle is relaxed without repetitive clutch work and continuous gear shifting.

Effortless Hill Starts

Automatic hill-hold and smooth torque delivery eliminate rollback anxieties on steep climbs toward Nuwara Eliya, Hatton, and Ella.

Focus on the Scenery

Keep both hands comfortably on the steering wheel and your focus on navigating Sri Lanka’s lush roadside panoramas and active wildlife areas.

Fuel-Efficient Japanese Tech

Equipped with modern electronic transmissions and hybrid powertrains (e-CVT) offering outstanding fuel mileage across expressways and regional routes.

Transmission Specifications & Fleet Overview

At KS Rent a Car, our core fleet of sedans (Toyota Premio, Toyota Allion, Toyota Axio, Toyota Prius), compact SUVs (Toyota Raize, Honda Vezel), compact hatchbacks (Suzuki WagonR, Toyota Aqua, Toyota IST, Honda Fit, Toyota Vitz), and passenger touring vans (Toyota KDH series) are equipped with dependable Japanese automatic transmissions (CVT, e-CVT, and multi-speed torque converters).

Transmission Details by Fleet Category:
  • Sedans & Hatchbacks: 100% Japanese automatic transmissions for effortless urban commuting and expressway cruising.
  • SUVs & Crossovers: Modern automatic CVT and hybrid e-CVT powertrains providing smooth hill climbs.
  • Passenger Vans: Toyota KDH flat roof, high roof, and standard touring vans feature automatic transmissions. Please note that select larger passenger vans such as the Nissan NV300 are available with manual or automatic options based on booking requirements and driver preferences.
