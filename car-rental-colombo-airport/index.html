How Colombo Airport Car Rental Handover Works

Arranging your car rental at Bandaranaike International Airport (CMB) with KS Rent a Car eliminates the usual airport travel stress. Instead of standing in long terminal counter queues after an exhausting international flight, our representative coordinates directly with your flight arrival time and meets you right outside the arrivals terminal in Katunayake.

1. Advance Coordination & Flight Tracking

Share your arrival flight number (e.g. UL, QR, EK, SQ, FZ) when booking. We track live flight radar data so our team is positioned on time, even if your flight lands early or experiences delays.

2. Curbside Meet & Greet Outside Arrivals

After baggage reclaim and customs clearance, step out into the arrival passenger pickup zone. Our team representative greets you with your vehicle ready in the immediate adjacent parking bay.

3. Fast 5-Minute Joint Condition Check

Together we walk through a transparent vehicle condition checklist, confirm fuel level, verify driving documents, and sign your rental agreement in minutes without hidden fees.

4. Immediate Expressway Access

The airport connects directly to the E03 Colombo-Katunayake Expressway. Drive into central Colombo in 25–30 minutes, or turn north along the coastal road to reach Negombo beach in 15 minutes.

Airport Location & Driving Distances

Bandaranaike International Airport is situated in Katunayake on Sri Lanka's western coast, providing convenient access to key travel destinations:

  • Colombo City Centre: Approximately 32 km south via the E03 Expressway (25–30 minutes drive).

Frequently Asked Questions: Colombo Airport Car Rental

Where do I meet the KS Rent a Car representative at Colombo Airport?

Our team meets you right outside the main international arrivals exit area at Bandaranaike International Airport (CMB). We coordinate via WhatsApp before you land and wait with your name board and reserved vehicle in the pickup parking area.

What happens if my arrival flight is delayed?

Because we track your live flight number, we automatically adjust our arrival timing. There are no penalties or cancellation fees for delayed flights; your vehicle and representative will be ready whenever your flight touches down.

Can I arrange airport car delivery for late-night or early-morning flights?

Yes. Many international flights arrive at CMB late at night or early in the morning. We coordinate vehicle handovers matching your confirmed flight arrival time upon advance reservation.

What driving licences are accepted for self-drive in Sri Lanka?

Under Sri Lankan motor traffic law, foreign tourists need an International Driving Permit (IDP) alongside their domestic licence, or a temporary driving permit issued by the Automobile Association of Ceylon (AAC). Contact our WhatsApp team prior to arrival for practical guidance on obtaining the local endorsement smoothly.

Can I pick up at Colombo Airport and return in Negombo or Colombo?

Yes, one-way handovers between Bandaranaike International Airport, Negombo beach hotels, and central Colombo addresses are fully supported. Simply specify your desired return location in the booking form or on WhatsApp.
