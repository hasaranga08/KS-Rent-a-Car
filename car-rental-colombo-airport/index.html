How Colombo Airport Car Rental Handover Works

Transparent, efficient, and personalized service without the hassle of airport desk delays.

1. Advance Coordination

Share your flight number and arrival time via WhatsApp or our enquiry form. We track your flight status for on-time vehicle readiness.

2. Meet Outside Arrivals

Our team representative greets you outside the terminal building with the vehicle. No long counter waiting lines.

3. Joint Condition Check

Complete a quick joint condition inspection, verify fuel level, review documentation, and receive the keys in minutes.

4. Immediate Expressway Access

From Katunayake, join the E03 expressway to reach Colombo in 25 minutes, or head north directly along the beach corridor into Negombo.
