Renting a Car at Colombo (Bandaranaike) Airport

Bandaranaike International Airport (CMB), commonly called Colombo Airport or Katunayake Airport, is the main international arrival terminal for travelers entering Sri Lanka. Located approximately 32 kilometers north of Colombo city center, navigating your onward journey immediately after landing is crucial for a relaxed vacation.

KS Rent a Car provides a seamless airport vehicle handover experience. Operating locally in Katunayake and Negombo adjacent to the airport, our pre-arranged service allows you to avoid lengthy counter queues and rigid airport desk waiting times.

The Practical 5-Step Airport Process:
  1. Contact KS Rent a Car: Message us via WhatsApp (+94 77 719 3915) or submit our web enquiry form.
  2. Provide Flight Details: Share your scheduled arrival date, flight number, and passenger count.
  3. Select Your Vehicle: Discuss self-drive or chauffeur options and required luggage room.
  4. Confirm Availability & Terms: We confirm vehicle availability, transparent pricing, and documentation needed.
  5. Smooth Handover at Airport: Our team coordinates directly with you upon touchdown for vehicle handover or chauffeured pickup.

Onward Routes from Bandaranaike Airport

Picking up your car at Katunayake Airport places you directly at the intersection of Sri Lanka's modern highway network:

  • Colombo City: Take the E03 Colombo-Katunayake Expressway directly into Colombo Port City and Fort (approx. 25–30 minutes).
  • Southern Beaches (Bentota, Hikkaduwa, Galle, Mirissa): From E03, connect immediately to E02 (Outer Circular) and E01 (Southern Expressway) without hitting city gridlock. Reach Galle in under 2 hours!
  • Cultural Triangle (Dambulla, Sigiriya, Anuradhapura): Head through Negombo or Giriulla to connect with the Kurunegala expressway route.
  • Kandy & Central Highlands: Fast connectivity via the Central Expressway (E04).
