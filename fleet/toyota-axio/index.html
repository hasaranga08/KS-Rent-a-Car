About the Toyota Axio

The Toyota Axio is one of Sri Lanka’s most dependable and practical family sedans. Combining proven Japanese engineering with generous cabin space, a smooth suspension setup, and effortless automatic driving dynamics, it delivers a comfortable journey whether navigating city traffic or embarking on cross-country road trips.

Equipped with a highly reliable 1.5L VVT-i petrol engine and smooth automatic transmission, the Axio handles Sri Lankan coastal highways and expressway commutes with outstanding fuel efficiency and low operating costs.

Recommended Use Cases

  • Airport Transfers: Spacious boot accommodates two large suitcases and extra hand luggage easily.
  • Island Touring & Excursions: Quiet cruising along the Southern Expressway and Colombo-Katunayake Expressway.
  • Business & Corporate Hire: Professional exterior styling and comfortable rear passenger seating.
  • Flexible Self-Drive: Smooth automatic gearbox and intuitive dashboard controls make driving relaxed.

Fuel & Performance Notes

Averages 12–15 km/L on open highways and 10–12 km/L in urban city driving.
