    About the Toyota High Roof

    The Toyota High Roof passenger van is engineered for premium comfort, dependable performance, and expansive interior room across Sri Lanka. With its elevated ceiling architecture, passengers enjoy easy cabin movement, generous headroom, and panoramic visibility throughout long scenic drives.

    Equipped with powerful dual air conditioning, plush seating, and dedicated luggage space, the Toyota High Roof is an ideal choice for group holidays, airport transfers, family celebrations, and corporate travel across Sri Lanka.

    Recommended Travel Uses

    • Islandwide Touring: Travel comfortably to popular destinations including Kandy, Sigiriya, Nuwara Eliya, Galle, and Ella.
    • Airport Pickups & Transfers: Seamless group transit from Bandaranaike International Airport (CMB) with ample space for passenger luggage.
    • Family & Group Functions: Spacious cabin capacity to transport extended families and groups together safely.
    • Corporate Transport: Professional, clean, and reliable presentation for business visitors and project delegations.
