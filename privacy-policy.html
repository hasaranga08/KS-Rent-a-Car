Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2026

1. Introduction

At KS Rent a Car Sri Lanka (operating at https://www.srilankacarrents.com), we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal information you share with us when inquiring about or booking vehicle rentals.

2. Information We Collect

When you contact us, request a quotation, or submit our online rental forms, we may collect:

  • Full Name
  • Email Address
  • Phone Number / WhatsApp Contact
  • Travel dates, flight arrival times, and pickup/drop-off locations
  • Vehicle preferences and group size
  • Driver identification (passport/driving license) required for rental agreements upon vehicle collection

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information strictly for:

  • Responding to your rental inquiries and preparing price quotes.
  • Coordinating airport transfers, vehicle delivery, and chauffeur assignments.
  • Fulfilling legal and vehicle rental agreement obligations in Sri Lanka.
  • Direct communication via WhatsApp or email regarding your travel schedule.

4. Data Protection & Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for commercial advertising. Your information is only shared with authorized staff or assigned drivers directly involved in providing your transportation service.

5. External Links and Third Parties

Our website uses WhatsApp integration links to facilitate direct communication with our reservations team. Please review WhatsApp's privacy policies when communicating through their platform.
