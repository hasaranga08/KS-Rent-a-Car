Your Transport Gateway in Katunayake

Katunayake is the central logistics and aviation nerve center of Sri Lanka. As the home of Bandaranaike International Airport (CMB) and the departure gate of the Colombo-Katunayake Expressway (E03), Katunayake allows you to reach Colombo city in just 25 minutes or immediately join the Southern Expressway network without navigating congested suburban streets.

Whether you are staying at an airport transit hotel (such as Gateway Hotel Katunayake, Amagi Aria, or local transit apartments) or arriving for business meetings in the Katunayake Export Processing Zone, KS Rent a Car provides rapid, punctual vehicle delivery and collection.

Expressway Accessibility: Katunayake gives you immediate access to E03 (Colombo Expressway), connecting seamlessly to the Outer Circular Highway (E02), the Southern Expressway (E01 to Galle and Matara), and the Central Expressway (E04 toward Kurunegala).
