Why Rent a Car in Negombo?

Negombo is widely regarded as the most practical arrival and staging town in Sri Lanka. Situated just 10 kilometers (approximately 15–20 minutes) north of Bandaranaike International Airport (CMB), most international travelers choose to spend their first night in Negombo to recover from long flights before setting off on their island adventures.

Renting a car directly in Negombo allows you to bypass the heavy traffic congestion of downtown Colombo. From Negombo, you have direct access to the Colombo-Katunayake Expressway (E03), the Central Expressway (E04) heading towards Kurunegala and Kandy, as well as the coastal roads leading north to Wilpattu and Kalpitiya.

Hotel Delivery Across Negombo: Staying at Jetwing Blue, Jetwing Beach, Heritance Negombo, or a boutique villa along Porutota Road? KS Rent a Car delivers your chosen vehicle right to your hotel reception or residence upon confirmed booking.

Frequently Asked Questions About Negombo Car Rental

Can I rent a car in Negombo and return it at the Airport?

Yes! One of the most popular arrangements is receiving your car at your Negombo beach hotel and returning it directly to Bandaranaike International Airport (CMB) at the end of your holiday.

What documents do foreign tourists need for self-drive?

You will need your home country driving licence along with an International Driving Permit (IDP) or Sri Lankan driving endorsement issued by the Automobile Association of Ceylon (AAC), plus your passport. Contact us on WhatsApp for assistance on obtaining the permit.
