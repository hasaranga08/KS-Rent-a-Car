Is Self-Drive in Sri Lanka Right for You?

Renting a car without a driver gives adventurous travelers absolute freedom. You decide when to wake up, where to take that unscripted detour down a palm-fringed coast road, and how long to stay taking photos of wild peacocks in Yala or mist over tea plantations in Hatton.

Driving in Sri Lanka offers a rewarding experience, especially with the modern expressway network (E01, E02, E03, E04) connecting the international airport, Colombo, Galle, Matara, and Kurunegala with smooth multi-lane highways. However, driving on regional two-lane roads requires alertness, defensive driving habits, and confidence with local traffic conditions (buses, tuk-tuks, and pedestrians).

Permit & Licence Requirements for Foreigners:
  • Your valid national driving licence from your home country.
  • An International Driving Permit (IDP) (1949 or 1968 convention). In Sri Lanka, foreign IDPs must be recognized/endorsed by the Automobile Association of Ceylon (AAC).
  • Alternatively, a temporary Sri Lankan driving permit can be obtained via the Department of Motor Traffic (DMT).
  • A valid passport with your Sri Lankan entry visa.
Need assistance with your driving permit? Connect with KS Rent a Car on WhatsApp and we will gladly guide you on the latest AAC endorsement steps.

Key Driving Rules in Sri Lanka

  • Drive on the Left: Traffic travels on the left side of the road; vehicles are right-hand drive.
  • Seatbelts: Compulsory for the driver and front-seat passenger at all times.
  • Expressway Speed Limits: Maximum 100 km/h on expressways (E01, E02, E03, E04).
  • Built-up Urban Areas: Usually 50 km/h; rural main roads up to 70 km/h unless otherwise signed.
  • Mobile Phones: Use of handheld mobile devices while driving is strictly prohibited by law.
