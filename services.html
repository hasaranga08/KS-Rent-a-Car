    Sri Lanka Car Rental Services

    Whether you need the freedom of self-drive or the convenience of a dedicated chauffeur, KS Rent a Car provides reliable vehicle solutions across Sri Lanka.

    1. Self-Drive Car Rental

    Explore Sri Lanka at your own pace with our self-drive car hire service. Self-driving gives you the freedom to set your personal schedule, stop at scenic viewpoints along the Southern Expressway or hill country roads, and discover secluded beaches and historical sites independently.

    • Choose from fuel-efficient compacts (Suzuki WagonR, Toyota IST, Honda Fit), comfortable sedans (Toyota Premio, Allion 260/240), or SUVs (Honda Vezel, Toyota Raize).
    • Clear rental agreements and transparent vehicle handover inspections.
    • Requirements: Valid domestic driving license and an International Driving Permit (IDP) or Sri Lankan driving endorsement for foreign visitors.

    • Ideal for holidaymakers, international tourists, corporate guests, and family holidays.
    • Available with executive sedans, crossover SUVs, or spacious Toyota KDH passenger vans for tour groups.
    • Customized routes, multi-day island tours, and inter-city transfers.

    • Timely arrival scheduled around your flight times.
