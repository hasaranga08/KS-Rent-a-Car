Family & Group Travel

Searching for 7-Seater Car Rental in Sri Lanka?

Many international visitors and families search Google specifically for a 7-seater car rental in Sri Lanka. While compact 7-seat vehicles exist in theory, they leave virtually no luggage boot space once all 7 seats are occupied. For family holidays with 4 to 6 large suitcases, strollers, and backpacks, traveling in a tight compact vehicle compromises comfort on multi-hour journeys.

At KS Rent a Car, our genuine family solution is the Toyota KDH Flat Roof (configured for 9 to 10 seats) or the Nissan NV300 (8 to 9 seats). When carrying a party of 6 to 7 passengers, these vehicles allow your entire family to sit comfortably with individual headrests, front-to-back dual air conditioning, and a generous rear cargo compartment that easily accommodates all suitcases without restricting passenger legroom.

Key Advantages for 6–7 Passenger Families:
  • Generous Luggage Capacity: Easily carries 5–7 large suitcases plus carry-on bags alongside 6–7 passengers.
  • Full Dual Air Conditioning: Overhead air vents ensure rear-row passengers remain cool across Sri Lanka’s tropical climate.
  • Reclining Individual Seats: Relax during 4-to-6 hour drives between Colombo, Kandy, Sigiriya, Ella, and Yala.
  • Underground & Parking Clearance: The flat roof profile enters hotel underground garages and tight parking areas without height restrictions.
